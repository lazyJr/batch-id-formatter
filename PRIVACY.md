# Privacy / 隐私说明

## English

Batch ID Formatter processes text that you paste into its popup locally on your device. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or store your text or personal data. It does not use analytics, remote code, or third-party services. The extension requests no browser permissions. When you click **Copy result**, the formatted text is written to your clipboard so you can paste it elsewhere.

For questions or reports, use the [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/lazyJr/batch-id-formatter/issues).

## 简体中文

批量编号格式化仅在设备本地处理你粘贴到弹窗中的文本。扩展不会收集、传输、出售或存储文本及个人数据，也不使用分析服务、远程代码或第三方服务。扩展不申请浏览器权限。点击「复制结果」时，格式化后的文本会写入剪贴板，供你粘贴到其他位置。

如有疑问或需要反馈，请使用 [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/lazyJr/batch-id-formatter/issues)。
